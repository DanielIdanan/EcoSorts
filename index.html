EcoSort

Reduce. Reuse. Recycle.

Snap a photo of any item and EcoSort tells you how to dispose of it — built for everyday use at home, school, or on the go, anywhere you're standing in front of a bin unsure where something goes.

What It Does

Sorts any item into Biodegradable, Hazardous, Recyclable, or Non-recyclable from a photo, and tells you exactly how to dispose of it — no manual lookup needed.

Where To Use It

Built for residents of Olongapo City — at home, at school, or anywhere you're standing in front of a bin unsure where something goes.

The AI

A waste-sorting model trained on real waste photos reads what each item is, right on your device — your photos never leave your phone or computer. Anything flagged hazardous gets a clear warning.
